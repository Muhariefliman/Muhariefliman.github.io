Engineering Journey

I am a Software Engineer based in Indonesia with a strong academic background from Bina Nusantara (BINUS) University, having completed both my Bachelor's and Master's degrees in Computer Science under the accelerated Master Track program.

My engineering philosophy revolves around building scalable, well-architected web and mobile systems while applying state-of-the-art Deep Learning models to solve complex real-world challenges like Computer Vision, Facial Recognition, and Character Classification.
